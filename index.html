01Manifesto

Not a genre exercise.
Not background worship.
Not disposable writing.

Hallowed Thunder treats Scripture as living dramatic material — incarnation, conflict, exile, mercy, judgment, memory, resurrection, fear, faith and the long silence between them.

The sound changes when the song demands it. The standard does not: writing first, composition first, truth before aesthetic.

The album on streaming now

Awakening

A New Testament album centered on Jesus — visceral, rock-rooted and deliberately unconcerned with staying inside one subgenre.

The songs follow the writing wherever it needs to go. The musical language can turn heavy, melodic, electronic, intimate or cinematic; the center remains the text and the composition.
